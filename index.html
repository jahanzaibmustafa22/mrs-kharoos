"Even the moon must have bowed in awe, on the day God created someone as breathtaking as you."

"Every lamp fades before the radiance of your face; when you smile, it feels like sighting the Eid moon."

"Grace in your eyes, sweetness in your voice, and light upon your face—truly, you are the loveliest masterpiece in this world."

"The flowers must have borrowed their fragrance from you, and the stars surely stole their sparkle from your eyes."
